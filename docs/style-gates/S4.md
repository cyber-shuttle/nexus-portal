### Token-arbitrary `[color:var(--nexus-red-*)]` destructive surface chrome

Three error-chrome sites (FailedQueue banner, PacketDetailDrawer error block, AdminDashboard alert StatCard) were rewritten through two iterations: first to `bg-destructive/5 + text-destructive`, then walked back on FailedQueue + PacketDetailDrawer + ErrorState because `--destructive` (= nexus-red-600 = `#dc2626`) on `bg-destructive/5` (≈ `#fdf4f4`) measures 4.46:1 — below WCAG AA. The spec (§7.5) explicitly allows the alt form `bg-[color:var(--nexus-red-50)] text-[color:var(--nexus-red-700)]`, which is the tokenized status-pill shape and keeps red-700 (`#b91c1c`) on red-50 well above AA.

Final state:

| # | Site | Form | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FailedQueue loud-age cell (`text-destructive`) | semantic | Text on white DataTable row — `#dc2626` on white = 5.93:1, passes AA |
| 2 | FailedQueue >24h banner surface | tokenized red-50/red-700 | Tinted surface — needs darker red-700 text for AA |
| 3 | PacketDetailDrawer last-error block | tokenized red-50/red-700 | Same — tinted surface |
| 4 | ErrorState container + icon | tokenized red-50/red-200/red-600 | Same — tinted surface; heading/message stay `text-foreground` / `text-muted-foreground` for contrast |
| 5 | AdminDashboard alert StatCard | `border-destructive/40 bg-destructive/5` semantic | Internal StatCard text is `text-foreground` (gray-900), not destructive — passes AA on the tinted bg |

All four destructive-chrome JSX sites carry an inline `//` comment explaining the tokenized choice.
