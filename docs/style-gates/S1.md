## Open items

These are **not S1 regressions** — they were present at the S0 head (`5894deb`) and were not flagged in the S0 gate. Filing them here so they roll into the appropriate later phase.

1. **a11y contrast: `text-muted-foreground` (#6b7280) on `bg-muted` (#f1f1f1) is 4.28:1 — below the 4.5:1 WCAG AA threshold.** Affects `StatusBadge` ("User" role pill etc.) on `/allocations/[id]?tab=users`, the Credit Transfer wizard chrome, and a few admin tables. Fix candidates:
   - Bump `--nexus-gray-500` slightly darker (e.g. `#5b6271`) — risky, ripples across all muted text.
   - Or scope a `bg-muted` darker token specifically for pill backgrounds.
   - Best resolved in **Phase S2** when `StatusBadge` is re-skinned (§7.2 already touches the inactive/expired variant tone).
2. **`tests/members.e2e.ts` is intermittently flaky under full-suite parallel load.** Passes at both S0 head and S1 head when run in isolation or with a smaller subset; observed to fail once during S1 verification and again at S0 head under similar conditions. Not consistently reproducible. Suggest a small selector tightening in the next phase that touches `MembersTab` (e.g. anchor the toast match to the Sonner notifications region instead of the page root).
3. **Sign-in page still uses `bg-nexus-gray-50`** — not in S1 scope (page is in `(auth)`, not the shell). To be cleaned in Phase S4 (hardcoded brand utility sweep).
4. **NeedHelpCard "Get Support" button has no click handler.** Spec says "no onClick for now; placeholder". A future feature should wire it to a mailto or support modal.
